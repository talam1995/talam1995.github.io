Master's thesis, supervised by Dr. Ritwik Bhattacharya and supported by the NSF LEAPS-MPS project *Prediction Issues in Progressively Censored Life-testing Experiments: New Ideas and Applications*. Nominated for the College of Science best thesis award.

The thesis studies prediction of future unobserved failure times from progressively Type-II censored data. It derives explicit analytical expressions for the joint best linear unbiased predictors (BLUPs) of two future order statistics under the D-optimality criterion, which minimizes the determinant of the predictors' variance-covariance matrix. It then compares joint and marginal predictors across censoring schemes using D-efficiency and trace-efficiency.
