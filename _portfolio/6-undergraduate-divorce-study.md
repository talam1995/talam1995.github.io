*University of Dhaka, B.S. in Statistics project*

*A Study on the Increasing Divorce Rate in Bangladesh: Causes, Consequences and Remedies*

For my undergraduate research project, I carried out a survey study from start to finish: designing the questionnaire, collecting primary data, and processing, analyzing, and interpreting the results. I used chi-square tests for bivariate analysis and fitted a logistic regression model to identify the determinants of divorce, which led to a set of policy recommendations.
