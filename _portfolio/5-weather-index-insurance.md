*Syngenta Foundation for Sustainable Agriculture, Bangladesh, Jul 2019 – Dec 2022*

Led the development of Weather Index Insurance (WII) products with international consultants, partner organizations, and stakeholders. Ran focus group studies, carried out actuarial analysis for existing and new products, prepared premium schedules and payout reports, and built the weather data pipeline from satellite sources and Automated Weather Stations.
