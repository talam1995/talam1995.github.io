*Research Cores Program, New Mexico State University, Sep 2025 – present*

My doctoral research develops methods for generating synthetic populations and synthetic microdata. The work integrates survey data with population-level information, applies calibration and validation techniques, and evaluates whether the resulting synthetic populations preserve important statistical relationships, so they can support causal and policy analysis.
