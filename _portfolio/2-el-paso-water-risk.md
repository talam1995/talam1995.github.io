*The University of Texas at El Paso, with El Paso Water, Jun – Aug 2025*

Developed a data-driven risk classification model that groups the groundwater wells feeding the Kay Bailey Hutchison Desalination Plant into low, medium, and high risk based on water quality indicators. The model helps prioritize high-risk wells for early intervention and monitoring, supporting sustainable water resource management.
