*The University of Texas at El Paso, 2025*

Contributed data analysis to *SIT2STAND: Validity in Stroke and Aging Populations in the Clinic and at Home*, which assesses sit-to-stand tests as a measure of mobility. Analyzed sensor-based and clinical data to study how mobility metrics relate to functional outcomes and to predictors of independent ambulation.
