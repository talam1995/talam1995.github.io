*The University of Texas at El Paso, Oct 2023 – Dec 2024*

As a Master's Research Assistant on the NSF-funded LEAPS-MPS project *Prediction Issues in Progressively Censored Life-testing Experiments: New Ideas and Applications*, I studied how to predict future failure times when a life-testing experiment is stopped early. I derived D-optimal joint best linear unbiased predictors for future order statistics under progressive Type-II censoring and compared joint and marginal prediction through D- and trace-efficiency. I also worked on simultaneous joint best linear unbiased prediction and joint best invariant prediction.

This work became my master's [thesis](https://scholarworks.utep.edu/open_etd/4219/), supervised by Dr. Ritwik Bhattacharya and nominated for the College of Science best thesis award.
