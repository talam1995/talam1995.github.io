Research Interests

I am a PhD student in Computer Science with a background in Statistics and Data Science. My research develops computational representations of complex populations through synthetic data generation, statistical modeling, causal inference, and simulation. I focus on building and validating reliable synthetic populations by integrating statistical and machine learning approaches to support data-driven analysis and decision-making.
